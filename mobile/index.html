Connecting for the first time
  1. Hold the lamp’s knob for six seconds until the entire lamp flashes blue.
  2. Tap “Find a lamp flashing blue” and select your lamp.
  3. Accept a Bluetooth pairing prompt if your phone shows one. No code is needed.

Pair within two minutes. Your phone remembers the pairing. Bluetooth control works without home Wi-Fi or internet. Hold for six seconds again to cancel pairing.

Wi-Fi, strip settings & firmware

These settings are available on the lamp’s existing web page. Hold the knob for three seconds, join its CoolLamp hotspot, and open http://192.168.4.1/ in your browser. On home Wi-Fi, use the lamp’s local address.
