    MAKE IT FIT

    Find the last LED

    Move a single teal light to the physical end of the strip. The position of that LED becomes your strip length—no counting needed.

    LED position
    LED position1

    Or turn the knob. If the light disappears past the end, move back until the last LED lights. Click the knob to save, or double-click to cancel. Setup cancels after 5 minutes without movement.

    Effect center

    Count from the data-input end. The center lies between this LED and the next. Each side scales independently. Save LED-count changes first. If the strip gets shorter, the center is limited to its last boundary.

    Microphone hardware

    Enable audio effects on lamps fitted with a microphone. Sound tuning lives with each audio effect.
