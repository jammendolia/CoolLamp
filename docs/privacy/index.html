CoolLamp

Privacy policy

CoolLamp is designed for local control. It does not require an account, contain advertising, or use analytics or tracking SDKs.

Effective date: November 4, 2026

This policy explains what the CoolLamp mobile app stores and communicates when you use it with compatible CoolLamp hardware.

Information kept on your device

The app may store lamp names, hardware identifiers, local-network addresses, rooms, groups, saved looks, display preferences, firmware status, and update or recovery state on your phone. Lamp access passwords and household access material are stored using the operating system's protected credential storage. This information is used to reconnect to and control the lamps you choose.

Bluetooth and local-network communication

CoolLamp uses Bluetooth and your local network to discover, pair with, configure, and control compatible lamps and bridges. Lamp names, identifiers, settings, status, and control messages can pass between your phone and your hardware. Home Wi-Fi credentials are sent only to the lamp you are configuring.

Sound-responsive effects

Sound-responsive effects are processed by a microphone installed in the lamp. The mobile app does not record microphone audio. Lamps may share locally derived sound levels or rhythm information with nearby lamps in the same lighting group; audio recordings are not saved or sent to the Internet.

Internet access

The app can contact the public CoolLamp release pages hosted by GitHub to check for and download lamp firmware. CoolLamp does not send your lamp names, lamp passwords, home Wi-Fi credentials, saved looks, or lighting activity with those requests. As with any Internet request, GitHub may receive ordinary technical information such as your IP address and browser or app request headers and processes it under GitHub's own privacy statement.

What CoolLamp does not do

  • No CoolLamp account is required.
  • No advertising or cross-app tracking is used.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting service is included.
  • No location history, contacts, photos, or microphone recordings are collected by the app.
  • Your CoolLamp information is not sold.

Removing information

You can remove a lamp from the app to delete its saved phone-side entry and protected lamp credential. Deleting the app removes its local app data according to the behavior of your operating system. A lamp factory reset is a separate physical action and clears the settings described by the app before you confirm it.

Children

CoolLamp is a general-purpose lighting controller. It is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If CoolLamp's data practices change, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosure will be updated before the affected version is released.

Questions

For privacy questions or support, visit the CoolLamp support page.
